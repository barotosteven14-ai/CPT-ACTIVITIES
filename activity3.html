Adobo

Adobo is considered one of the national dishes of the Philippines.

Abodo Recipe:

Ingredients:

  • 1 kg chicken (cut into serving pieces)
  • ½ cup soy sauce
  • ½ cup vinegar
  • 1 cup water
  • 6 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 teaspoon whole black peppercorns
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil
  • 1 teaspoon brown sugar (optional)
  • Salt to taste (if needed)

Instructions:

  1. Marinate the chicken in soy sauce and garlic for 30 minutes.
  2. Heat the oil in a pot and lightly brown the chicken.
  3. Add the remaining marinade, vinegar, water, bay leaves, and peppercorns.
  4. Bring to a boil. Do not stir for the first 2–3 minutes after adding the vinegar.
  5. Lower the heat and simmer for 30–40 minutes, or until the chicken is tender.
  6. Add brown sugar if you prefer a slightly sweeter flavor.
  7. Continue cooking until the sauce thickens to your liking.
  8. Serve hot with steamed rice.
